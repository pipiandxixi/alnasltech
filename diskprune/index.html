DiskPrune

Reclaim space from AI work. A Mac app built for Macs that run AI apps and AI development.

AI work leaves a lot behind: model downloads, transcripts and edit history from coding assistants, generated files, build output and temporary files. DiskPrune has special scanning rules for exactly these, so it can free far more space than a general-purpose cleaner finds. Everything you choose is reviewed first, goes to the Trash by default, and can be restored.

  • Scanning is always free. A free 7-day trial lets you remove files; after that, a one-time purchase unlocks removal on all your Macs. No subscription.
  • Everything is checked on your Mac. File names and sizes are never uploaded.
